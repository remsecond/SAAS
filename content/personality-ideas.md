# Hallway side notes: idea bank

Anyone can add a line here. Nothing in this file is shown in the app. A line goes live only when someone
copies it into `personality.json` with `"status": "approved"` after a read for voice, accuracy, placement
and unintended meaning. The rules live in `PERSONALITY-SPEC.md`.

The short version of the voice: joke about the app, time, the universe and the general absurdity of
school. Never about a student, grades, missed work or anyone real. A fact needs a checked source.
